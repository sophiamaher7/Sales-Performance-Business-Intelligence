# Business Questions

This document summarizes the business questions addressed by the Sales Performance & Business Intelligence solution.

## Executive Management

1. How is the business performing overall?
2. What are the current revenue, cost, profitability, order, and return metrics?
3. How does current performance compare with the previous year?
4. Which KPIs are achieving their assumed targets?
5. Which KPIs are underperforming?
6. How is performance changing over time?

## Sales Management

1. How are sales changing over time?
2. Which stores have the strongest performance?
3. Which employees contribute most to sales?
4. Which products and subcategories have the highest and lowest demand?
5. Which countries contribute most to sales?
6. What is the average order value?
7. Which areas require further investigation?

## Marketing

1. How is marketing engagement performing?
2. How many clicks are being generated?
3. How does marketing performance change across selected time periods?
4. How does the selected period compare with the previous year?

## Promotions

1. How many promotions are available during a selected month?
2. Which promotions are active during the selected period?
3. How does promotion availability change over time?

## Delivery & Operations

1. How many orders are scheduled for delivery within the next 1 week?
2. How many are scheduled within the next 15 days?
3. How many are scheduled within the next 1 month?
4. How many are scheduled within the next 6 months?

## Detailed Analysis

1. Which countries or business areas require deeper investigation?
2. What additional information can be obtained through drill-through analysis?
3. How can users move from high-level KPIs to detailed operational analysis?

---

## Analytical Approach

The questions were translated into:

* Data preparation requirements
* Data-modeling requirements
* DAX measures
* KPI calculations
* Time-intelligence calculations
* Dynamic filtering
* Interactive visualizations
* Drill-through analysis

The objective was to create a BI solution that answers business questions rather than simply displaying data.
